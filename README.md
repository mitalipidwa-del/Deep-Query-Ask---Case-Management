# Deep-Query-Ask---Case-Management
Deep Query Ask – Salesforce Service Cloud Case Management Demo

Welcome to the official video demonstration for the Deep Query Ask project! This video walks through the end-to-end Salesforce Service Cloud case management solution designed to streamline customer support, optimize ticket routing, and enhance resolution tracking.

🎥 Video Overview & Demo Highlights
In this video demonstration, we cover:
Business Context & Problem Statement: Understanding the challenges in managing customer support requests and how Salesforce Service Cloud addresses them.
Case Intake & Routing Automation: Exploring automated case creation, assignment rules, and queue management to ensure swift routing to the right support agents.
SLA Tracking & Escalation: Demonstrating how service level agreements (SLAs) and automated escalation rules maintain high service standards.
Reporting & Dashboards: Reviewing analytical reports and executive dashboards built to track case metrics, agent performance, and resolution times.
Business Analysis Artifacts: A quick look at the requirement-gathering process, functional requirements, and structured documentation supporting the build.

🛠️ Key Salesforce Technologies Demonstrated
Salesforce Service Cloud & Case Management
Object Manager & Security Controls (Profiles, Roles, Sharing Settings)
Process Automation (Assignment Rules, Queues, Escalation Rules)
Reports & Dashboards for CRM Analytics
Business Analysis (BRD, FRD, User Stories, Acceptance Criteria)
